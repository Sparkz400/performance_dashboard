# Roadmap

[Factory readiness](https://github.com/Sparkz400/performance_dashboard/blob/main/docs/FACTORY_READINESS.md)
adds scoped supervisor line/action workflows, attributable maintenance corrections,
fair comparisons of frozen shift reports and full-application endurance testing.
Short runs do not establish multi-day reliability; physical qualification remains open.

The proposed direction is a dependable, self-hosted view of new and existing
industrial equipment, starting with one company, one line and a reconciled shift.
Current compatibility and qualification limits still apply.

The plan has six stages:

1. Trustworthy evaluation: measurement correctness, clear feature boundaries and recovery.
2. One-line pilot: commissioning, shifts, downtime reasons and useful operator workflows.
3. Connectivity and dependable history: selected gateways/protocols, retention and integrations.
4. Production improvement: quality, batches, maintenance, energy and asset context.
5. Multi-site operations: site permissions, identity and verified edge synchronization.
6. Evidence-backed intelligence: governed analytical questions and demand-led extensions.

Stages are acceptance gates, not promised release dates. The core remains read-only
toward machines; physical-machine qualification is separate from automated checks.
